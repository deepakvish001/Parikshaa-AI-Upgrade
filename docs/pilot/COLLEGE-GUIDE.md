# Parikshaa: your first test, step by step

*(हिंदी में: [COLLEGE-GUIDE.hi.md](COLLEGE-GUIDE.hi.md))*

Parikshaa runs online tests for your students: on a phone or a lab computer,
in English, Hindi and nine other Indian languages. Answers are saved as
students type, so a dropped connection loses nothing. After a written answer,
it may ask one short follow-up question — how well a student explains their
own answer is shown to you beside the marks, as evidence for you to weigh,
never as a verdict.

Your console: **https://exam.yourdomain.in/console/**

## 1. Make your account (5 minutes)

1. Open the console and choose **Make your institution's account**.
2. Give your institution's name as students should see it, your name, your
   work email and a password of at least 12 characters.
3. You are signed in at once. We email you a link: **open it to confirm your
   address** (check spam if it has not arrived in a few minutes). Until you
   do, you can write and try tests, but not invite students or colleagues.

Your first **25 attempts are free** — enough for a pilot batch.

## 2. Look around (10 minutes)

- A short **tour** starts when you arrive; you can take it again from Home.
- On Home, choose **Load the sample exam**, then **Try it as a candidate**:
  you sit a 20-minute aptitude paper exactly as a student would. Nothing is
  recorded.

## 3. Write your test

Choose one:

- **New exam** — type questions in: multiple choice (one or several right
  answers, with negative marking if you use it), numbers, written answers, and
  code. Set the time and, if you want one, a qualifying mark.
- **Import questions** — upload a Word question paper or a spreadsheet into
  the question bank, check what was found, then build the exam from the bank.

Press **Try test** on the exam to sit it yourself before anyone else does.

## 4. Invite students

On the exam's **Candidates** tab:

1. **When candidates can take it** — set when it opens and closes (IST).
2. **Invite from a spreadsheet** — a sheet with each student's roll number,
   name and email (and mobile number, for SMS or WhatsApp). **Download a sample sheet** shows the
   columns. Check the preview, then invite.

Each student gets their own link by email (and SMS or WhatsApp, if you tick them), then a
reminder the day before and another an hour before the test opens. The same
tab shows whether each message went; **Download links** gives you every link
to share another way.

**If the date changes**, change it in the same place and press **Reschedule**.
Every invited student who has not begun moves to the new time, with their
reminders, and is told the new time; their link stays the same.

Add colleagues from **Staff** (after confirming your email): authors write
tests, invigilators watch sittings, reviewers read results.

## 5. On the day

Open the exam's **Live** tab. You see every student and whether they are
still connected.

- **A student's connection drops**: nothing is lost. They open the same link
  again — on any device — and carry on.
- **A student needs more time**: their row's **⋯ → Extra time…**, with a
  reason. For a power cut, **Extra time for everyone** gives everyone still
  sitting the same minutes at once.
- **Tell everyone something**: **Announce**. It appears on every open test.

**A test in the computer lab.** On the same tab, **Lab mode → Change**:

- **Only from the lab's network**: press **Add it** beside "This computer's
  address" while sitting in the lab (ask your IT team for the full range if
  the lab has several). A student who opens their link anywhere else is told
  the test can only be taken in the exam room.
- **Candidates wait for the invigilator**: students who open their link see
  "Waiting for the invigilator" until you press **Start the test for
  everyone**. Each student's time starts when they begin, not before.

## 6. Results

On the exam's **Results** tab, press **Grade all**: objective questions are
marked at once, written answers within seconds each, with progress as it
goes. The same tab shows scores, who qualified, and how the batch did;
**Download Excel** or **CSV** for your records, or **Email to me** to get
the workbook in your inbox. Each student's report shows
their answers, marks and any follow-up, with the reasoning behind every mark.

The model marks written answers first. To read them yourself, **Written
answers → Start marking** on the Results tab shows them one by one without
names; keep each mark or change it.

When you are ready, **Send results to candidates…** sends each graded
student their marks by email, and by WhatsApp where it is set up (and, if you
choose, whether they qualified). It says first how many it will reach. From
then, each student's own link shows their marks question by question and a
scorecard PDF with a QR code anyone can scan to check it. For a week they may
ask for a recheck of up to three questions; the requests appear on the
Results tab, where you give the mark as it should stand and a reply. Nobody is
sent theirs twice, so you can send again after grading the rest.

After submitting, each student is asked how the test went and whether
anything got in the way. Answering is optional; **What candidates said** on
the Results tab shows the ratings and comments, without names.

## 7. Across the term

**Insights** in the sidebar shows every student across your tests by roll
number — their percentage in each, their average and how they have moved —
and lists questions worth a second look before you use them again.
